A stand of trees

A few trees are planted, and more arrive every year. Each grows at a rate of its own, more slowly as it gets bigger, and is felled once it reaches a certain size. How many trees will there be, and how much timber, once the stand has settled down?

Here it is as a Simile model. The large box is a population: what is inside it is the model of one tree, and the model has as many trees as the population holds at the time, each with its own values.

Inside the box, size grows through growth, at a rate gr that is drawn at random for each tree, between 0.1 and 0.3, when it appears. x and y say where the tree stands, also at random. The three symbols along the top of the box are how trees come and go: initial creates the first ones, recruitment adds more each year, and death removes a tree once its size passes a threshold. Outside the box, total adds up the sizes of all the trees there are.

The four circles on the edge of the diagram are the model's parameters: initial_trees and recruitment_rate, the trees planted at the start and each year; max_size, the size growth slows towards; and death_size, the size at which a tree is felled.

What it does

The model ran as this page opened, for 60 years. Each line is one tree, from the year it arrived to the year it was felled; the label at the end says how many there are.

At first the stand fills up, and the total rises. After about 17 years the fastest growers reach the felling size and start to go; by about 35 years trees are leaving as fast as they arrive, and from then on the stand holds at around 50 to 60 trees, wandering about a level it keeps to. Because each tree's growth rate is drawn at random, each run is a little different.

Where they stand

The same trees, each standing where it is, as tall as its size. Drag across the picture, or use the sliders above it, to look from another side.

Try it

Move a slider and the displays above follow it: the model runs again, here in the page, every time the slider moves. The model itself is not changed: model values puts the sliders back.

You can also change the model itself. Double-click any element of the diagram to see and change its equation, and the model runs again with your change. Nothing you do here is kept: Reset model puts it back, and so does coming back to this page.

This model is built step by step in the Trees tutorial (Stage 4); here its constants are drawn out as parameters, and trees are felled at size 7 rather than 17, so the stand settles within 60 years rather than 300, with tens of trees rather than hundreds. The model runs in your browser, compiled by Simile; no server is involved.
